ABOUT US

Premium Car Detailing stands for mobile automotive care at the highest standard – treating every vehicle as if it were a collector piece. Behind Premium Car Detailing stands master technician Slavisa Vujic.

 

Our focus is centered on clean, precise, and durable surface restoration, that not only delivers stunning aesthetics, but actively preserves the long-term value of your vehicle. Ob thorough interior & exterior cleaning, paint correction, sealants, or ceramic coatings: We combine Swiss precision standards with prompt mobile execution, delivering results that are visible, tangible, and long-lasting.

What sets us apart is our autonomous mobile concept. We arrive directly to you. You save travel time, fuel, and scheduling hassles. While you proceed with your day, we care for your vehicle on site. Fully mobile means fully equipped: with independent onboard power and purified water, professional studio gear and a mobile setup that performs reliably outdoors. If a garage or carport is available, we can also work indoors depending on weather and requirements.

 

 Currently, we operate primarily in Aargau as well as across neighboring regions deploying with a clear focus on expanding our reach. We serve Private Clients as well as Corporate Fleets – from daily drivers to exotics, from family SUVs to luxury flagships and vintage classics. Our standard is uncompromisingly premium: Every vehicle deserves professional Care, protection, and long-term value retention – regardless of make or vehicle class.

 

 Our quality promise is built upon three pillars: Cleansing, Protection, and Perfection. Cleaning & Decontamination means thorough, material-safe, and clinically structured cleaning – inside and out. Protection means not just beautifying surfaces, but fortifying them against daily hazards: UV rays, road grime, salt, water spots, and micro-wear. Perfection is the sum of all details: crisp edges, rich finishes, optical clarity, and results that impress for years to come.

Included are Trust, Transparency, and Discretion core principles of how we work. You always know what is being done, why it is done, and the exact benefits it provides. We operate with absolute respect for your vehicle and environment, dedicated to one goal – maximum customer satisfaction without empty promises.

 

 Premium Car Detailing is for individuals and companies who value an immaculate appearance, preserved value, and a stress-free experience. Mobile service with us means: showroom results with zero effort on your part.

 

 

We provide honest, transparent consultation ( Contact ), which service makes the most sense for your vehicle and daily driving – from regular upkeep to high-grade ceramic coatings.
