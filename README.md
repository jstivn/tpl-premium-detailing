# TPL-PREMIUM-DETAILING | Luxury Mobile Auto Detailing Template

> Fully autonomous, high-converting luxury mobile car detailing & ceramic coating landing template. Adapted 1-to-1 from Swiss market leader `premiumcardetailing.ch` into English, with all original HD media, videos, and before/after comparisons preserved locally.

---

## 🚀 Key Specifications
- **Stack:** Pure HTML5 + Tailwind CSS + Vanilla JavaScript (Zero build step, 0.2s Cloudflare edge speed).
- **Typography:** Plus Jakarta Sans (Headings) & Inter (UI/Body).
- **Palette:** Ultra-luxury Pure Black (`#000000`), Dark Zinc (`#09090b`), Electric Cyan (`#00bcff`), and 24K Gold Rating Accents (`#eab308`).
- **Assets:** 9 Local 1080p/720p MP4 videos + 68 Local High-Res Before/After images in `assets/`.
- **Pages:**
  - `index.html` — Full-featured master high-conversion landing page.
  - `mobile-detailing.html` — Dedicated mobile detailing & interior shampoo service page.
  - `paint-ceramic.html` — Certified multi-stage paint correction & 9H ceramic coating page.
  - `auto-glass.html` — Mobile windshield replacement & ADAS calibration page.
  - `gallery.html` — Visual portfolio & transformation gallery.
  - `about.html` — Mobile studio equipment specs & philosophy.
  - `faq.html` — Comprehensive 12-question help center.
  - `contact.html` — Dispatch contact hub & direct inquiry form.

---

## 💎 Signature Interactive Features
1. **Fullscreen Video Hero Background:** 1080p high-pressure rinse & foam wash loop with dark ambient gradient overlays.
2. **Interactive Before & After Comparison Slider:**
   - Touch & drag draggable divider handle with smooth mouse/touch tracking.
   - 4 Instant Switcher Tabs: Severe Scratches vs Polished, Interior Leather Rejuvenation, Swirl Removal, Hood Reflection Revival.
3. **Bento Grid Core Services:**
   - Mobile Auto Detailing (Hand wash, steam extraction, leather conditioning).
   - Paint Correction & 9H Ceramic (Multi-stage rotary compounding, 5-year warranty).
   - Mobile Auto Glass Replacement (OEM glass, $0 insurance claim handling).
4. **Live 4K Video Reels Showcase:**
   - 4 vertical high-framerate reels featuring actual detailing work.
   - Interactive play/pause and mute/unmute audio toggle buttons.
5. **Dynamic Instant Price Calculator:**
   - Vehicle size selector (Coupe, Mid-Size SUV, Large SUV, Exotic).
   - Package selector (Refresh, Detail, Concierge).
   - Add-on checkboxes (Ceramic coating, Engine steam clean, Headlight restoration, Auto glass).
   - Live total estimate recalculation.
6. **Conversion Triggers:**
   - Mobile Sticky Bottom Action Bar with Direct Call (`tel:`) and Online Booking.
   - Verified 5.0 Star Google Rating badge with authentic translated client reviews.
   - Top announcement bar with active mobile unit pulse animation.
