Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about our mobile studio, ceramic warranties, or insurance glass replacement? Find transparent answers below.

Do I need to supply water or an electrical outlet?

No. Our mobile studio is 100% self-contained with reverse-osmosis deionized water tanks and silent onboard inverter generators. We need zero hookups from your property.

How long does a ceramic coating truly last?

Our professional-grade 9H ceramic coatings last between 3 to 5 years depending on package selection and maintenance habits. We provide a detailed warranty certificate and aftercare guide.

How do insurance claims work for cracked windshields?

Send us your insurance details and policy number. We interface directly with your insurer (State Farm, GEICO, Progressive, Allstate, Zurich, etc.) and handle billing directly. In almost all policies with glass coverage, there is zero deductible.

Can you detail my car at my corporate office or workplace?

Yes! We service executive parking structures and office plazas daily. Our clean water recovery protocols leave zero messy runoff or chemical puddles.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Zelle, bank wire transfer, and commercial corporate invoicing.
