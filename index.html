Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our premium mobile vehicle detailing

We come directly to you – home, office, or private garage. Our self-contained mobile lab brings everything: deionized water, electric power, and professional studio gear. You need to prepare nothing.

No, that is not necessary at all. Our mobile setup is 100% autonomous. We bring our own purified water reservoir and silent power generators, enabling us to work anywhere without external utility connections.

Duration depends on the selected service. A basic exterior wash takes approx. 2–3 hours; a full interior and exterior reconditioning with machine polish takes 4–6 hours. Ceramic coatings require additional time for preparation and curing.

Machine polishing removes scratches, holograms, and oxidation to bring the paint to a mirror finish. A ceramic coating shields the paint long-term against environmental fallout, UV rays, and grime – creating a durable, hydrophobic protective layer for up to 5 years.

Yes, exactly. That is our entire philosophy. We arrive at your preferred location – private residence, corporate parking, or country club. You save travel time and continue your day uninterrupted.

We service all vehicle types: sedans, SUVs, sports exotics, collector classics, vans, and motorcycles. Each vehicle is individually treated with bespoke products and safe techniques suited to its specific paint and materials.

Reach out via phone, WhatsApp, or email. We review your requirements, create an individualized quote, and schedule an appointment – flexibly around your calendar.

A professional ceramic coating lasts 3–5 years with proper maintenance. Wax coatings provide 3–6 months of protection. We gladly advise you on the best solution for your vehicle and driving habits.

Costs vary depending on vehicle size, condition, and service package. Basic exterior detailing starts at $160; complete packages with machine polishing and ceramic coating start at $650. We gladly provide an individual estimate.

Our mobile units deploy across the entire regional metropolitan area. Contact us for custom locations – we always make it happen.
